# ADR 0004: Surgical Single Follow-Up with Safe Decline & Concurrency Safety

* **Status:** Accepted
* **Date:** 2026-10-16
* **Deciders:** Shane Christian (Track A), Madhav Thesiya (Track C), Meet Virugama (Track B)
* **Target Components:** `backend/app/services/followups.py`, `backend/app/services/pipeline.py`, `backend/app/services/diagnosis_store.py`, `backend/app/api/followups.py`
* **Related Requirements:** Riddle 3 ("The Question That Separates"), System Invariants #4, #5, #7

---

## Context

When initial evidence is ambiguous (e.g., competing stories with overlapping confidence), educational software typically adopts one of two flawed approaches:
1. **Unbounded Conversational Chatbots:** The student is trapped in an open-ended multi-turn dialog. This induces severe testing fatigue, anxiety, and unpredictable response latency, while frequently leaking the canonical answer or leading the student.
2. **Forced Interrogation Without Agency:** The student is forced to guess through additional questions, penalizing timid learners and contaminating diagnostic telemetry.

Furthermore, in a live classroom, a critical **concurrency race condition** emerges:
While an AI background job is re-evaluating a student's follow-up reply, the teacher in the front of the room may simultaneously click an authoritative override (e.g., *"This student told me verbally they understand"*). If the slower AI request writes to the database second, it risks overwriting the teacher's authoritative judgment.

---

## Decision

We resolved these pedagogical and distributed systems challenges through four core engineering mechanisms:

1. **Strict Single-Turn Budget ($\le 1$ Follow-up):**
   - The platform permits **at most ONE** follow-up check per initial response.
   - The guardrail records whether a follow-up has already been issued. Once answered or declined, no further AI follow-ups can be scheduled for that response under any circumstance.

2. **Pedagogical Guardrails on Candidate Generation:**
   - Candidate follow-up questions generated by AI (`followup-v2`) are passed through deterministic regex filters in `services/followups.py`:
     - **Length Filter:** Maximum 30 words to maintain minimal cognitive load.
     - **Leak Prevention:** Rejects any question containing the canonical answer or problem solution.
     - **Neutrality Check:** Strips accusatory, leading, or judgmental wording.
     - **Shortest Preference:** Among valid candidates that discriminate between the competing hypotheses, the algorithm selects the most concise option.

3. **Safe, One-Click Zero-Penalty Decline:**
   - A student can decline the follow-up with a single click using one of three explicit reasons:
     - `skip` ("Skip for now")
     - `not_sure` ("I'm not sure")
     - `teacher` ("I'd rather ask my teacher")
   - Declining creates **zero penalty**, does **not alter answer correctness**, and invokes **zero synthetic AI calls**. The state is safely closed as `not_sure`, preserving all competing hypotheses for teacher review.

4. **Atomic Concurrency Control & Teacher Override Supremacy:**
   - We implement pessimistic row-locking (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on PostgreSQL, serial transactions on SQLite) when updating diagnosis records in `services/diagnosis_store.py`.
   - Diagnoses use monotonic integer versioning (`MAX(version) + 1`) protected by a strict database constraint:
     ```sql
     UNIQUE(response_id, version)
     ```
   - When an AI re-read finishes, it verifies that the diagnosis version it was analyzing is still the active head. If a teacher override arrived in the interim, the AI write is discarded. **The teacher override remains permanently authoritative.**
   - Unfinished replies remain idempotent and recoverable via `POST /api/follow-ups/{id}/resume`.

---

## Consequences

### Positive
- **Guaranteed Student Comfort:** Students never feel trapped by an algorithmic interrogation loop.
- **Predictable Request Lifetimes:** Eliminating multi-turn loops guarantees that student assessment sessions complete within tight, predictable time windows.
- **Data Integrity Under Concurrent Teacher Edits:** In live classroom demonstrations with high teacher activity, no teacher decision is ever overwritten by an asynchronous background AI task.

### Negative / Trade-offs
- **One-Shot Diagnostic Bound:** If a single follow-up reply still fails to resolve the ambiguity, the system cannot ask a second clarifying question; it must defer directly to human teacher inspection. (This is a deliberate pedagogical feature, not a bug).

---

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Multi-Turn Adaptive Socratic Dialog:**
   - *Rejected:* Causes classroom friction, exceeds mobile student attention spans, risks hallucinated prompts, and introduces unbounded latency.
2. **Last-Write-Wins (LWW) Without Versioning:**
   - *Rejected:* Causes silent data corruption when a delayed LLM completion overwrites a real-time teacher manual override.
