## Scope

- One optional, untimed diagnostic check per initial response.
- `followup-v2` proposes a question and up to two alternatives in one call, with rationale and predicted observations.
- `services/followups.py` filters length, repetition, direct answer leaks, judgement and incompatible story IDs; shortest eligible candidate wins.
- Older question-only model output receives code-owned hypothesis confirmation hints. All predicted observations remain teacher-only proposals, not actual student evidence.
- Hypothesis-specific template or teacher review when candidates are unusable; never a generic repeat explanation solely to consume the question allowance.
- `decline` accepts `skip`, `not_sure`, or `teacher`; no AI call or fabricated reply. Choice and closure are atomic, repeat-safe, preserve original correctness/history, and cannot supersede a teacher or replace a saved reply.
- Student controls and teacher explanation panel use the same typed API. Saved-reply recovery never offers a second reply box.
- Additive idempotent schema upgrade gives existing follow-ups empty metadata and keeps all existing data.

## Acceptance checks

- Candidate filtering and shorter alternative selection; unknown misconception IDs and identical predicted observations rejected.
- No useful safe fallback means `not_sure`, not a repeated question.
- Same initial error with different replies can produce different supported interpretations; unclear replies retain uncertainty and do not trigger another follow-up.
- Teacher-only rationale/predictions never reach student endpoints.
- All three decline choices, refresh recovery, idempotency, ownership/validation, reply-versus-decline interleaving, teacher before/after decline, and old-schema migration.
- Offline backend suite; frontend production build; browser checks at desktop and 360 px.

## Limits

Word count approximates burden, not actual learner time or measured information gain. Pattern checks do not prove semantic safety or diagnostic value. Model-generated likelihoods are not calibrated probabilities. A successful model-free test does not establish live Gemini quality, hosted Postgres concurrency safety, or deployed health. Predictions must never be copied into observed evidence.
