# Riddle 4 implementation and handoff

Date: 2026-10-17. Base: `origin/main` at `dee26c0` (Riddle 3 merged). Branch: `riddle-4-transfer`.
Contract: [API v1.5](../../api-contract.md#re-checks-riddle-4--does-the-understanding-travel-and-last). Verification: [status](../../status.md).

> Better Marks, Better Understanding? You offer a next step, and the next score rises. Perhaps the idea became clear. Perhaps the pattern became familiar. Perhaps the learner just got used to your tool. Change the situation, take the tool away, come back in a month: does the understanding survive? What would convince you that someone has learned something, not just got better at your questions, and what do you change when it didn't travel?

## Scope

- **Transfer items** (`transfer_items` table): the same key idea in a changed situation, teacher-written, with a `change` note. Authored in the quiz or added later (allowed after students answered: no evidence changes). The sample quiz ships two per question with different kinds of change; numeric answers are checked arithmetically.
- **Re-checks** (`rechecks` table): scheduled by the teacher per question for everyone who answered (or listed students), opening after `delay_days`. Each student gets a transfer item they have never seen, preferring a new kind of change. One waiting re-check per student and question.
- **Unaided attempt:** `POST /api/rechecks/{id}/answer` runs the same rules + AI pipeline with follow-ups disabled. The student sees no hints, nothing about the original, and no result.
- **Verdict** (`services/transfer.py::judge`): wrong → `did_not_travel`; right but the key idea isn't shown, or the AI was unavailable → `unconvincing`; right with the key idea → `travelled`, or `lasting` once the gap since the first answer reaches `RECHECK_LASTING_DAYS` (7).
- **What changes** (`change_plan`): chosen from the verdict and, for `did_not_travel`, the story behind the new miss, naming the previous step that didn't carry over.
- **Teacher view:** results page panel per question (schedule, better marks vs better understanding, every re-check with verdict and plan); response detail lists that student's re-checks. **Student view:** an open check-in appears after the quiz is done.

## Acceptance checks

`tests/test_riddle4_transfer.py` (19 tests): authoring rules; items hidden from students; adding a situation after answers; delayed re-checks hidden until open; default next step from the teacher's remedy; never the same item twice, plus a new kind of change; only answered students scheduled; travelled vs lasted (10-day gap); right without reasoning is not understanding; no AI means not learned; the returning mistaken rule changes the approach; no follow-up during a re-check; ownership, once-only and not-yet-open; response detail; class delete cascade; sample items.
`tests/browser_riddle4.cjs` with `tests/browser_riddle4_app.py` (local only, scripted model, in-memory DB): teacher schedules via the UI, the student answers the unaided check-in and sees no verdict, the teacher sees the verdict and change plan; 360 px and 1280 px with no horizontal overflow.

## Limits

- A "month" can't be demonstrated live: a same-day re-check is honestly labelled "Travelled — check again later". The gap uses the server clock and the first answer's timestamp.
- Whether a transfer item really changes the situation is the teacher's judgement; code only rejects an identical prompt.
- Trigger answers don't carry over to new numbers, so on a re-check a mistaken rule is identified from the explanation (AI), not from the answer pattern.
- New tables are created on existing databases by `create_all`; hosted Postgres not yet verified. No live-Gemini check of re-check readings yet.
